Your blog is ready. Everything you need is one click away, and there is nothing to install or configure.

<!--more-->

## Write something

Click **New post** in the admin and start typing. Use the toolbar to make text bold, add headings, lists and quotes, or paste a picture straight into the page. Prefer typing symbols? Switch to **Markdown** at the top and write that way. Your post is the same either way.

## Make it yours

Open **Settings** to change the blog name, tagline, time zone and more. Pick **Download a backup** there whenever you want a safe copy of everything.

## What happens behind the scenes

- Your posts are saved as simple files, so nothing can get stuck in a database.
- Readers get a fast, clean page with an RSS feed, tags and search built in.
- Drafts stay private until you press **Publish**, and a future date schedules a post.

When you are ready, delete this post and write your own.
